PeakPower · Proof of Concept

Trading POC — live demo

A browser-only, client-calculated stand-in for two PeakPower portals: the customer-facing Consumption/Trading portal and the internal Back Office trade desk. Everything runs client-side against static test data — there is no backend and no real customer or market data behind this demo.

Consumption & Trading

Dashboard, Connections, Consumption, Prices, Trading and Wallet — request a hedge trade block against a metering connection and track its offer.

Back Office

Trade Desk

Price incoming trade requests, send firm offers back, and confirm accepted trades from the internal desk queue.

Open Back Office →
⚠️ Open both in two tabs of the same browser. The two portals hand trade requests and offers to each other through the browser's local storage, on this one site — so a trade submitted on the Customer Portal only shows up on the Back Office desk if both are open in the same browser, same device (not across two different browsers, devices, or private-browsing windows).
Demo / proof-of-concept only — synthetic test data throughout, no authentication, no real trades or funds. Data resets whenever this browser's local storage is cleared. Source: github.com/htthinh1999/peakpower-trading-demo
