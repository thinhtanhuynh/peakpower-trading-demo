PeakPower · Proof of Concept

Trading POC — live demo

A browser-only, client-calculated stand-in for PeakPower: the onboarding flow a company signs up through, the customer-facing Consumption/Trading portal, and the internal Back Office trade desk. Everything runs client-side against static test data — there is no backend and no real customer or market data behind this demo.

Demo only. All data on this site — customers, connections, trades, prices, wallets — is synthetic test data generated for this proof of concept. It does not represent real customers, accounts, or transactions.

⚠️ Open both in two tabs of the same browser. The two portals hand trade requests and offers to each other through the browser's local storage, on this one site — so a trade submitted on the Customer Portal only shows up on the Back Office desk if both are open in the same browser, same device (not across two different browsers, devices, or private-browsing windows).
